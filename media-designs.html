Websites, social media and branding for small businesses and clubs. Based in Jackson, Michigan; not limited to it. One person, answers his own phone.

I build websites for small businesses and clubs that don't have one — and fix the ones that stopped getting updated in 2012.

Most of the shops, contractors, restaurants and clubs I talk to are busy being good at the thing they actually do. The website is the part nobody got to. That's fair. It's also costing them work, because everybody under fifty checks a phone before they call.

GMx Media + Designs is the shop side of GMxTARRANT — same person, same outfit, same email. If you got here from the overland side of this site, that's why the name changed.

How This Works

You See It Before You Commit

Nobody should have to imagine what they're buying.

Where To Start

Tell me about your business and I'll show you what it could look like — before you commit to anything. No meeting to sit through, no proposal to read, nothing to sign to find out whether we're a fit.

I'd rather show you than describe it. If what I put in front of you isn't right, you say so and we're done, no hard feelings.

What I Do

Three Things

You're hiring a person, not an agency. My name, my number, my email — not a ticket number, not an account manager who changes every six months, not a support queue where you explain your own business from scratch every time you call.

Small Business Websites

A real website, built to work on a phone first. Menus people can read, forms that actually reach you, photos of the work you've done. Built for businesses and clubs that have nothing, or something that hasn't been touched in a decade.

Hand-written, so there's no WordPress underneath it and nothing that needs patching every few weeks just to keep working.

Hosting is handled — at the traffic a local business actually gets, it costs nothing to run, and I set it up in your name so it stays that way.

And it doesn't go stale. New hours, a new menu, a price change, a photo of the job you finished last week — send me a message and it's on the site in a day or two. Weekly, if that's what your business needs. You're not filing a ticket and waiting a month to fix a typo.

You own the files, the domain and the accounts. Nothing is locked to me — if you ever want somebody else to take it over, they can.

Social Media Management

Posting, replies and monthly content for businesses and clubs that know they should be on Facebook and Instagram but never find the hour. You keep doing the work — I make sure people see it.

Logos, Branding & Print

Logos, menus, signage, business cards, truck lettering, club patches and decals. The stuff that has to look like it belongs to the same business as everything else. Built as real vector art at the size it's getting cut or printed — and in a version that still reads at profile-picture size, because a mark that turns to mush when it's small isn't finished.

Three Things I Find On Almost Every Local Site

It was built for a desktop computer. Most of the people looking at it are on a phone, turning it sideways, giving up.

There's no way to reach you but a phone number. Plenty of people will send a message at 9pm who will never make a call at 9am. That's a lost job you never knew existed.

The menu or price list is inside a photo. Google can't read a photo, and nobody can forward one to their spouse and have it be useful.

How It Goes

Three Steps

No discovery calls, no proposals with stock photos in them.

  1. You tell me what you've got. Use the form below, or just say "we don't have a website." That's enough to start from.
  2. I show you what it could look like. You look at it on your phone before anything is agreed. If it's not right, you say so.
  3. If you like it, we talk price. One number, not a range that mysteriously grows. If it's not something I should be doing, I'll say so and point you somewhere better.
Being Straight With You

This is not my full-time job. I work days, I've got two young kids, and I take on a small number of projects at a time so the ones I do take actually get finished. If I'm booked up I'll say so rather than string you along.

I'm also not the right call for everything. Online stores with hundreds of products, anything needing logins and user accounts, apps — that's somebody else's job and I'll tell you that too.

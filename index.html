Who We Are

No $150K Build. No Sponsors Telling Us What to Say.

We're Josh and Kyla from Jackson, Michigan. We've got two young kids, a Bronco Outer Banks we call DA_GRN_M4CHINE, and a trailer we're building in the garage one weekend at a time.

Most off-road content is somebody's $150,000 rig in Moab. That's fun to watch and completely useless if you're trying to figure out whether you can take your own family out on a Saturday without wrecking anything or ruining everyone's day.

So that's what we make. Every trail guide answers the same questions: what does it cost, what do you actually need, is it doable with a mostly-stock rig, and can you bring your kids. When something goes wrong, that's in the video too — that's usually the useful part.

We're active in the Michigan Bronco Club and you'll find us at most of the runs. Come say hi.

People ask about the build constantly, so we put the whole list in one place — every part on the truck and why we run it.

Michigan Trail Guides

Every Trail, Honestly Reviewed

Permits, drive times, what breaks, where to camp, and whether you can bring the family. One trail at a time.

St. Ignace, MI · Easy–Intermediate

St. Ignace Area

Our family favorite. Over 500 miles of trails explored with forest roads, water crossings, sand, mud, and endless places to explore in the eastern U.P.
Drummond Island, MI · Intermediate–Advanced

Drummond Island

One of Michigan's best true off-road destinations. Rocks, ledges, mud, water, and technical trails make this one worth the ferry ride.
West Branch, MI · Beginner–Intermediate

West Branch Area

Sand, mud, forest roads, and plenty of two-track. A great family riding area that can completely change after rain or snow.
Mears, MI · Beginner–Advanced

Silver Lake Sand Dunes

Michigan's ultimate sand playground. Big dunes, steep climbs, bowls, and plenty of room to make the day as mild or wild as you want.

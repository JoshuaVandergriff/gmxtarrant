# Silver Lake Sand Dunes

**Overall Rating:** 5/5  
**Difficulty:** Easy to Difficult — you decide how hard you want to push it  
**Family Friendly:** 5/5  
**Solo or Buddy:** Solo is fine, but a buddy is recommended if you're going to push the vehicle.

## Quick Take

Michigan's ultimate sand playground. Big dunes, steep climbs, bowls, and plenty of room to make the day as mild or wild as you want.

## About the Area

Silver Lake is completely different from every other place on this list.

There aren't really trails.

You have hundreds of acres of open sand dunes where you can cruise around, climb hills, hit bowls, explore, and pretty much decide how aggressive you want your day to be.

That's what I love about Silver Lake.

You can take a relatively stock four-wheel-drive vehicle onto the dunes, air down, and have an awesome day without doing anything crazy. Or you can start looking for bigger climbs and more challenging terrain.

It's completely up to you.

## Trail Conditions

It's sand.

But sand changes constantly.

Expect:

- Loose sand
- Steep dune climbs
- Bowls
- Whoops
- Drop-offs
- Wind-blown ridges
- Heavily churned-up sand

Temperature, wind, and how many vehicles have been out there can completely change how the dunes feel.

A hill you drove up easily in the morning might be significantly more torn up by late afternoon.

## Solo or Buddy?

**My recommendation: Solo is fine, buddy recommended if you're going to push the vehicle.**

Silver Lake is probably the place on this list where I'm most comfortable being out with one vehicle because you're in a controlled riding area with plenty of other people around during busy weekends.

That doesn't mean you can't get stuck.

If you're planning on trying bigger climbs, exploring the softer areas, or pushing your vehicle harder, I still like having another rig with us.

For a normal family day on the dunes, though, I don't consider having a second vehicle mandatory.

## Air Down

Air down your tires.

This is probably the single biggest mistake I see people make at Silver Lake.

Lower tire pressure gives you a much larger footprint in the sand and makes an unbelievable difference in traction.

The Michigan DNR recommends approximately **12–15 PSI**, although your ideal pressure depends on your tires, wheels, and vehicle weight.

Make sure you have a compressor with you because you'll want to air back up before getting back on the road.

## Permits and Equipment

Silver Lake has more requirements than a normal Michigan trail ride.

Before heading onto the dunes, make sure you have the required:

- Michigan ORV License
- Michigan ORV Trail Permit
- Recreation Passport
- Orange dune safety flag
- Proper seat belts
- Required rollover protection
- Required safety equipment

I recommend checking the current Michigan DNR Silver Lake rules before every trip because requirements can change.

## My Advice

For your first trip, don't immediately follow the guy on 40s launching over dunes.

Spend some time learning how your vehicle reacts in the sand.

Maintain momentum going uphill, avoid stopping halfway up a climb, and remember that the other side of a dune can be much steeper than the side you're looking at.

And when you crest a dune, don't blindly send it over the top.

Silver Lake is also one of the best places we take the family because you aren't stuck crawling down a trail all day. We can wheel for a while, stop and hang out, watch other vehicles, grab food, and head back out.

It's basically a giant off-road playground.

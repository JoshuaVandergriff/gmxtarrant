This is everything that's actually on the truck. Not a wish list, not stuff we were sent to review — the parts we paid for, bolted on, and have run through mud at West Branch, sand at Silver Lake, and limestone on Drummond Island.

The build is deliberately mild. A 2" lift and 35s on a mostly-stock Outer Banks that still has to do school runs on Monday. If you're wondering whether you can do this without a second vehicle and a $40,000 build budget — this page is the answer.

What We Always Carry

Every trail guide on this site says the same thing, so it belongs here too: kinetic rope or recovery strap, soft shackles, tire repair kit, air compressor, basic tools, and offline maps. That list doesn't change whether we're forty minutes from home at Bundy Hill or deep on Drummond.

One Rule We don't recommend anything we haven't run for a season. If a part is on this page, it's been on the truck or in the tent through Michigan weather and Michigan trails. That's the whole standard.

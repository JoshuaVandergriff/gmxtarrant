Cadence

Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 August 2026

Cadence is a personal social-media analytics tool operated by GMX Tarrant. This policy explains what it collects, where that information lives, and how to remove it.

The short version

Cadence runs on the account holder's own computer. Data retrieved from connected social platforms is stored in a file on that computer and is not transmitted to GMX Tarrant, to any server we operate, or to any third party. There is no analytics, no advertising, and nothing is sold.

What we access

When you connect an account, Cadence requests read-only permission to retrieve the following through each platform's official API:

CategoryExamples
Profile informationUsername, display name, avatar, follower and following counts
Your own postsPost ID, caption or title, publish time, duration, link, thumbnail
Performance metricsViews, likes, comments, shares, saves, reach, average watch time

Cadence requests read-only access. It cannot post, delete, edit, message, follow, or change anything on a connected account, and it does not request the permissions that would allow it to.

Cadence only retrieves data belonging to the account that authorised it. It does not access other people's accounts, private messages, or content you have not posted.

Where it is stored

  • All retrieved data is written to a database file on the account holder's own computer.
  • Access tokens issued by each platform are stored in that same local file so Cadence can refresh data without asking you to log in repeatedly.
  • Nothing is uploaded to GMX Tarrant. There is no account to create, no server-side copy, and no cloud sync.
What that means in practice

Because everything is local, the security of your data is the security of your computer. Anyone with access to that machine and its files can read what Cadence has stored, including the access tokens. Treat it as you would any file containing personal information.

How long it is kept

Cadence records a snapshot of your metrics each time it runs, and keeps those snapshots indefinitely so it can show change over time. Nothing expires automatically. The account holder decides how long to keep it, and can delete it at any point.

Deleting your data

  • Remove everything: delete the data folder inside the Cadence directory, or use the application's built-in reset command. This permanently removes all stored posts, metrics and tokens.
  • Revoke Cadence's access at the platform itself, which works regardless of what is on your computer:
    • TikTok: Settings → Security & permissions → Manage app permissions
    • Instagram: Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites
    • YouTube / Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions

If a copy of your data is ever held by GMX Tarrant — which is not the case for the local application described here — you may request its deletion using the contact address below, and it will be removed within 30 days.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade or share your data. There are no advertising networks, no analytics services, and no third-party processors involved in the local application. The only parties your data passes between are your computer and the social platform you connected, over that platform's own API.

Children

Cadence is not directed at children and is not intended for anyone under the minimum age required by the connected platform's own terms.

Platform relationships

Cadence is an independent tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by TikTok, Meta, Instagram, Google or YouTube. Data is accessed only through those platforms' official public APIs and subject to their terms, and each platform's own privacy policy governs the data while it is in their hands.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated. Material changes affecting how data is handled will be described here rather than made silently.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or requests relating to your data: gmxtarrant@gmail.com

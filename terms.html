What Cadence does

Cadence connects to social media accounts you own or manage, retrieves your own performance data through each platform's official API, stores it on your computer, and presents it as a report with observations about posting patterns.

Your responsibilities

  • Only connect accounts you own or are authorised to manage.
  • Comply with the terms of each connected platform. Cadence's permission to access those platforms is granted by them and can be withdrawn by them.
  • Keep your own credentials secure. Cadence stores access tokens on your computer; protecting that computer is your responsibility.
  • Do not use Cadence to collect data about people who have not authorised it.

What Cadence will not do

Cadence requests read-only access. It does not post, delete, edit, message, follow, or otherwise modify anything on a connected account, and it does not request the permissions required to do so.

Accuracy and interpretation

Read this one

Cadence reports figures supplied by each platform's API and derives observations from them — for example, that posts published in a particular window have historically performed better than your own average.

These are observations about past performance, not predictions and not advice. Social platforms change how content is distributed constantly and without notice. A pattern that held last month may not hold next month, and a recommendation drawn from a small number of posts may be coincidence. Cadence labels the sample size behind each observation so you can judge for yourself. Decisions about what and when to post remain entirely yours.

Platform data itself is sometimes delayed, revised, or incomplete. Cadence reports what the API returns and cannot correct errors originating upstream.
